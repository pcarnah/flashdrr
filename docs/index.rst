
Welcome to FlashDRR's documentation!
====================================

`FlashDRR` provides differentiable DRR (Digitally Reconstructed Radiograph)
raycasting and volume rendering for PyTorch. Turn CT volumes into synthetic
X-ray projections while keeping gradients flowing through both the volume and
the camera parameters.
